<h1>Lorenzo Sciarretta</h1>

<div class="intro">
  <div>
    <p>I research how multiple large language models can work together: coordinate, agree, and reason as a team. I'm currently doing this at the <a href="https://disco.ethz.ch/">DISCO Lab</a>, ETH Zürich.</p>
    <p>I'm finishing a joint Master's in Computer Science at ETH Zürich and the University of St. Gallen. My work sits between multi-agent LLM systems, weight-space learning, and post-training. I like systems that learn how to learn, models small enough to live on a chip, and clean abstractions that survive contact with reality.</p>
    <p>I'm open to collaborations. If you work on post-training of LLMs, on the emergent behaviours that appear during pre-training and post-training and how they relate to model architecture, or on cooperative AI, I'd be glad to hear from you: <a href="mailto:lorenzo.sciarretta@unisg.ch">write me an email</a>.</p>
