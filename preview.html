ABOUT // who I am, what I do

I'm an AI/ML researcher and engineer finishing a joint Master's in Computer Science at ETH Zürich and the University of St. Gallen.

My work sits at the intersection of multi-agent LLM systems, weight-space learning, and post-training. I like systems that learn how to learn, models small enough to live on a chip, and clean abstractions that survive contact with reality.

Before this I worked on hyper-representations across collections of neural networks at the HSG AI/ML Lab, neuromorphic edge ML at the ETH AI Center, and quantitative time-series research at the HSG Financial Econometrics Chair.
